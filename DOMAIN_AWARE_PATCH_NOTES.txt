Domain-aware v3 redesign

The previous v2 patch is obsolete. It conditioned the LSTM only on left/right
InterPro ID embeddings in the initial recurrent state. That architecture was
sparse, did not generalize to unseen pairs, and could ignore the conditioning.

v3 conditions on terminal domain sequences, recurrently observed domain IDs,
domain lengths, and predicted target linker length. The context is injected at
every decoder step. Dataset construction, validation splitting, generation,
structural scoring, and analysis were rewritten accordingly.

See README.md and MIGRATION_NOTES.md. Old checkpoints must be retrained.
